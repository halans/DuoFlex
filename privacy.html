Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short: DuoFlex doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account and no analytics, and the app doesn't connect to the internet. Everything it does happens on your iPhone, and your photos go only to your own photo library.

What DuoFlex accesses, and why

DuoFlex asks for permission before it uses anything that needs it, and works with as little as possible.

AccessWhat it's used for
Camera Showing the live image in the viewfinder and taking photos. The live image is processed on your iPhone and never stored or sent anywhere.
Photos (add only) Saving each photo you take to your photo library, along with its RAW file if you turn RAW on. DuoFlex can add photos but can't see, read, or change anything already in your library.
Location (optional, while using the app) Recording where you took each photo in that photo's details, so you can find it on a map in Photos. DuoFlex keeps only your most recent location in memory while the camera is open, and doesn't store or share it. If you don't allow location access, everything else works the same.
Motion sensors Drawing the level and tilt indicator. Readings are used only while the level is on screen and are never stored.

What's stored on your iPhone

  • Your photos, in your photo library, managed by the Photos app.
  • LUT files you import, copied into DuoFlex's own storage on your device so they're available next time. Delete one from the app's Menu, or delete the app to remove them all.
  • A few preferences, such as your frame format, film, and grid, whether you've seen the introduction, and your place on the current roll.
  • Film profiles, created in DuoFlex's temporary storage when you open the Menu, so they're ready to export. They leave your iPhone only if you choose to share them.

Like other app data, this may be included in your iPhone's backups, such as an iCloud Backup, under the backup settings you choose.

What's in your photos

Each photo, and its RAW file if you save one, includes standard camera details, such as the device model, lens, exposure, and date, plus the name of the film simulation or LUT you used. If you allowed location access, it also includes where the photo was taken.

These details travel with a photo when you share it. To share without location, tap Options in the share sheet in Photos and turn off Location.

What DuoFlex doesn't do

  • It doesn't connect to the internet, so it can't send your photos or any other data anywhere.
  • It doesn't include analytics, advertising, or tracking, or any third-party code that does.
  • It doesn't ask you to create an account or give your name or email address.
  • It doesn't sell or share data, because it doesn't collect any.

Your choices

You can change camera, Photos, and location access at any time in the Settings app, under Apps → DuoFlex. Turning off location stops new photos from being tagged; photos you've already taken keep their details until you edit them in Photos.

Children

DuoFlex doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If DuoFlex's handling of your information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
