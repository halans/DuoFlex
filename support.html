Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Getting started

How do I hold iPhone Duo?

Hold it in portrait and half-fold it to about 90°, with the camera half standing up and facing your subject. The bottom half lies flat and becomes the viewfinder. Hold it at your waist and look straight down to frame, just like a twin-lens reflex.

Why does DuoFlex ask me to turn to portrait or open the device?

The camera is laid out across the fold of iPhone Duo's inner display, which works in portrait. On the outer display, or with the inner display in landscape, DuoFlex shows a message instead. Open the device and turn it to portrait to start shooting.

Can I use DuoFlex on an iPhone that isn't iPhone Duo?

Yes. Tap Use Camera Anyway on the message that appears, and DuoFlex stacks the settings on the top half of the screen and the viewfinder on the bottom. Hold your iPhone in portrait. DuoFlex remembers your choice.

Shooting

How do I change exposure or focus?
  • Exposure: swipe up or down on the viewfinder, in ⅓-stop steps from −3 to +3 EV. You can also tap the EV value on the status screen.
  • Focus: tap the AFc tile and choose MF, then swipe left or right on the viewfinder to focus nearer or farther. Sharp edges are outlined in yellow.
What's the difference between WIDE, 3:2, and SQUARE?

WIDE fills the whole viewfinder and saves the photo with exactly that framing. 3:2 and SQUARE (6×6) save classic film frames. Choose a format with the tabs at the top of the status screen.

How do I focus on a particular spot?

Tap it on the viewfinder. DuoFlex focuses and sets exposure there, and a brass square shows where. This works the same with every lens, including 2×.

Can I tap to focus in manual focus?

No. In MF, focus moves only when you swipe left or right on the viewfinder, so a stray touch can't undo the distance you've set. With Auto ISO, a tap still sets where exposure is metered. With a fixed ISO as well, a tap has nothing to set, so no square appears. For quick focusing on a spot, switch back to AFc.

What is roll mode?

Turn on Shoot in Rolls of 24 in the Menu (the ≡ tile) to work like film: once you start a roll, its film stays loaded for 24 exposures. The EXP counter shows your place on the roll, and a notice tells you when it's finished. To switch film part-way through, open the film sheet and tap Load New Roll.

Can I use Camera Control?

On iPhones with Camera Control, press it to take a photo, then slide to adjust exposure compensation or choose a film. During a roll, the film stays fixed. iPhone Duo uses the volume buttons as a shutter instead.

Can I take a photo without touching the screen?

Yes. Press a volume button to fire the shutter, or set the self-timer from its tile for a 2 or 10 second delay. Press the shutter again during the countdown to cancel it.

How do I turn off the dark edges in the viewfinder?

Open the Menu (the ≡ tile) and turn off Ground-Glass Viewfinder. This only changes what you see while framing; your photos aren't affected.

Is there help inside the app?

Yes. Open the Menu (the ≡ tile) and tap Shooting Guide for a summary of every gesture and control. It works without an internet connection.

Film and LUTs

What kinds of LUT files can I import?

DuoFlex imports 3D LUTs in the .cube format, from 2 to 128 points per side, as exported by apps such as DaVinci Resolve and Lightroom. 1D LUTs aren't supported. Tap the LUT tile, then Import .cube File, and choose a file from the Files app.

How do I remove a LUT I imported?

Open the Menu (the ≡ tile). Your imported LUTs are listed there, each with a delete button.

Do film simulations change my photos?

Yes. The film simulation and LUT you choose are applied to the saved photo, exactly as you see them in the viewfinder. Choose Standard and no LUT for an unaltered image. If you also save RAW, the DNG stays unprocessed.

How do I save RAW photos?

Tap the HEIF tile and turn on Also Save RAW (DNG). Each photo is then saved as a RAW+HEIF pair: Photos shows the photo with your film look, and editing apps can use the unprocessed DNG. The DNG is always the full, uncropped frame. RAW needs a camera that supports it; if it doesn't, the switch is unavailable.

How do I get the film look on my RAW files?

Open the Menu (the ≡ tile) and tap Export Film Profiles to save or AirDrop a .cube profile for each film simulation. Apply one to your DNG in an editor that supports LUTs, such as Lightroom Classic, Photomator, Darkroom, or DaVinci Resolve. Profiles carry the color; grain and halation aren't included. Each DNG also notes which look it was shot with.

Photos and permissions

Where do my photos go?

Straight to your photo library, in the Photos app. The latest one also appears on the last-photo tile.

The viewfinder is black, or photos aren't saving

DuoFlex needs camera access to show the viewfinder, and Photos access to save. Open the Settings app, go to Apps → DuoFlex, and make sure Camera is on and Photos is set to Add Photos Only or Full Access.

How do I stop photos from recording my location?

In the Settings app, go to Apps → DuoFlex → Location and choose Never. Everything else keeps working. See the Privacy Policy for details.
